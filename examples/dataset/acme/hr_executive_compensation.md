# ACME Corporation — Executive Compensation and Severance Structure

**Document ID**: DOC-HR-002  
**Department**: Human Resources  
**Classification**: RESTRICTED  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Audience**: Board of Directors, Compensation Committee, C-Suite Only  

## 1. Executive Equity Grant Schedules

Executive Vice Presidents and above receive annual Restricted Stock Unit (RSU) awards subject to the following 4-year vesting schedule:
- Year 1 (Cliff): 25% of granted RSUs vest 12 months after grant date.
- Years 2–4: 6.25% vests quarterly thereafter, contingent upon continuous active employment.
- Performance Stock Units (PSUs) vest based on Total Shareholder Return (TSR) relative to the S&P 500 index. A 75th percentile ranking triggers a 150% multiplier.

## 2. Executive Severance and Change of Control Provisions

Under the Tier-1 Executive Severance Plan:
- In the event of involuntary termination without Cause, the Executive receives 24 months of base salary paid in lump sum, plus pro-rated annual bonus at target.
- A "Double Trigger" Change-in-Control event accelerates 100% of unvested equity if termination occurs within 18 months following an acquisition.
- Golden parachute gross-up provisions are explicitly excluded; excise tax reduction will apply if net proceeds to the executive are higher.

## 3. Discretionary Executive Perquisites

The Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer are authorized up to $120,000 annually for private charter flights for business-related domestic travel, subject to audit by the Compensation Committee.
