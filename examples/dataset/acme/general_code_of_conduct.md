# ACME Corporation — Global Code of Business Conduct and Ethics

**Document ID**: DOC-GEN-001  
**Department**: General  
**Classification**: PUBLIC  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Audience**: All Global Employees, Contractors, and Partners  

## 1. Respect and Anti-Harassment Standards

ACME Corporation is dedicated to fostering a workplace free of discrimination, harassment, and retaliation. Mutual respect is expected in all interactions. Discrimination based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or veteran status is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate termination.

## 2. Gifts, Entertainment, and Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid business arrangements or relationships that conflict with the company's best interests:
- Nominal promotional items valued under $50 USD (e.g., pens, notepads) may be accepted.
- Gifts or hospitality exceeding $50 in value must be disclosed and approved by the Ethics and Compliance Committee.
- Giving or accepting cash or cash equivalents (gift cards) is strictly forbidden under all circumstances.

## 3. Whistleblower Protections and Reporting Channels

Employees who observe violations of law, regulations, or this Code are encouraged to report them promptly through the confidential ACME Ethics Hotline (1-800-555-ACME) or ethics@acme.internal. Reports may be filed anonymously. Retaliation against any individual reporting in good faith will not be tolerated.
