# ACME Corporation — Customer Support Escalation Matrix and Refund Authority

**Document ID**: DOC-SUP-001  
**Department**: Customer Support  
**Classification**: INTERNAL  
**Effective Date**: January 10, 2026  
**Version**: 2.3  

## 1. Support Tiers and Response Times

Customer inquiries are triaged based on severity:
- **Severity 1 (Critical System Outage)**: Response SLA within 15 minutes. 24/7 on-call engineering dispatch.
- **Severity 2 (Major Feature Degraded)**: Response SLA within 1 hour. Engineering resolution within 8 hours.
- **Severity 3 (Minor Defect / Question)**: Response SLA within 4 business hours. Resolution in next release cycle.

## 2. Refund and Goodwill Credit Limits

Support representatives hold discretionary authority to issue customer refunds and billing credits according to their tier:
- **Tier 1 Support Agent**: Up to $50 per customer per incident without supervisor approval.
- **Tier 2 Support Specialist**: Up to $250 per customer per incident.
- **Support Team Lead / Manager**: Up to $1,000 per incident.
- Any goodwill concession exceeding $1,000 requires written sign-off from the Director of Customer Success and VP of Finance.
