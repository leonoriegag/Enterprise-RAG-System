# ACME Corporation — Corporate Travel Expense and Per Diem Guidelines

**Document ID**: DOC-HR-003  
**Department**: Human Resources  
**Classification**: INTERNAL  
**Effective Date**: January 15, 2026  
**Version**: 2.1  

## 1. Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem)

For authorized domestic business travel within North America and Europe:
- The standard daily meal allowance is $75 USD per full travel day.
- Partial travel days (departure or arrival days) are reimbursed at 75% of the standard per diem ($56.25 USD).
- Note: This policy supersedes the outdated 2024 memo which stated a $50 per diem limit.

## 2. Hotel Lodging Caps

Employees traveling for business must book accommodations through the Concur travel portal. Maximum nightly room rates (excluding local taxes) are capped at:
- Standard metropolitan areas: Up to $220 USD per night.
- High-cost markets (New York, London, San Francisco, Tokyo): Up to $350 USD per night.
