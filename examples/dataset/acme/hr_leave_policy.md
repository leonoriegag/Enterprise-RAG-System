# ACME Corporation — Global Employee Leave Policy

**Document ID**: DOC-HR-001  
**Department**: Human Resources  
**Classification**: INTERNAL  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Version**: 3.2  

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time permanent employees at ACME Corporation accrue paid time off based on their tenure:
- **Years 0–2**: 20 business days per calendar year (accrual rate of 1.67 days per month).
- **Years 3–5**: 25 business days per calendar year (accrual rate of 2.08 days per month).
- **Years 6+**: 30 business days per calendar year (accrual rate of 2.50 days per month).

PTO must be submitted through the Workday portal at least two weeks in advance for absences lasting five consecutive days or longer. Immediate manager approval is required.

## 2. Rollover and Forfeiture Rules

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any carried-over days must be utilized before March 31 of that year, or they will be permanently forfeited without financial compensation.

## 3. Sick and Medical Leave

ACME provides up to 10 paid sick days annually. Sick leave is front-loaded on January 1st and does not carry over across calendar years. For absences exceeding three consecutive working days, employees must provide a medical certificate signed by a licensed healthcare practitioner to People Operations.

## 4. Parental and Family Care Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's arrival.

## 5. Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 consecutive paid business days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Up to 3 days of paid leave are granted for extended family members.
